# ROOTED specialty matrix: Agriculture

The truth model for what each kind of business can do on ROOTED Agriculture, what it must prove before it goes live, and what is missing. Built from `agriculture.json` by `build.py` (do not edit this file by hand). As of 2026-11-27; the "required now" column was checked against the live database that day.

Canonical home: rooted-platform: matrix/agriculture/specialties.json.

Public copy: the per-type gaps (what is not yet enforced) are kept in the private platform repository, not here.

## How to read it

- **required now**: Badges the live database asks for before a business of this type can go live (public.specialty_badge_requirements_v2).
- **required target**: What ROOTED should ask for. LICENSED comes from an approved license or food permit file, INSURED from an insurance file, safety from a food safety certificate; files are checked automatically when the business name is on them and the end date is ahead (M415).
- **recommended**: Asked for or shown as a badge, never blocking.
- **state notes pa**: Plain-language reminders for Pennsylvania, where ROOTED starts. Not legal advice: rules change and vary by county and state.
- **check window**: How long the buyer has to check goods before the seller is paid: 24h perishable, 48h standard, 3 business days for orders of $2,000 or more (rooted_policy.order_check_window_settings_v1).

## Plans and fees

- Vendors: Premium $79/month, Premium Plus $149/month (20% off yearly); team seats $15 (Premium) or $10 (Premium Plus) a month, first seat free.
- Institutions (Premium / Premium Plus a month): school $125 / $250; university $175 / $325; hospital $275 / $425; jail $225 / $375; nonprofit $75 (Premium only); business $79 / $149; care $125 / $250.
- Free: listing, shop (5 items), events.
- Premium: bulk market, shop (25 items).
- Premium Plus: RFQ bidding (vendors), RFQ posting and awards (institutions), shop (100 items).
- ROOTED service fees: bulk offer 3%, at most $150; rfq bid 3%, at most $150; shop listing 6%; event ticket 5% + $0.50; edu offering 2.5%; event spot 0%; plans 0%; card processing about 2.9% + $0.30, carried by the seller.

## Who can buy

- Institutions: school, university, hospital, jail, nonprofit, business, care.
- Also: members (shop).
- Kinds of buyer business: INSTITUTION_KITCHEN, RESTAURANT, GROCERY_STORE, CATERER, FOOD_PROCESSOR, DISTRIBUTOR, CARE_FACILITY, FOOD_HUB, FOOD_COOP, MARKET.

### Farm (`FARM`)

- Groups: PRIORITY_PRODUCER, STANDARD_VENDOR. Section: AGRICULTURE_FOOD. Can also: rfp b2g, waste, events, event vendor spots, field trips.
- Required now: Insurance, Farm badge, Verified. Should require: Verified, Farm badge, Insurance.
- Recommended: safety (GAP or food safety plan) for school and hub buyers.
- Pennsylvania: Raw, whole produce sold by the grower usually needs no food license in PA; washing, cutting or packing it does (PA Dept of Agriculture retail or wholesale food license). Schools and food hubs often ask for GAP or a food safety plan. Eggs: PA egg handling rules apply above small-flock limits; keep at 45°F.
